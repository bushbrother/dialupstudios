Privacy Policy

Out of Office, by Dial-Up Studios · Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Out of Office (the "game") uses, why, and the choices you have. Dial-Up Studios ("we", "us") is responsible for the game and is the data controller under UK GDPR.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account with us, and we don't run our own servers that collect data about you.
  • Your game progress is stored on your phone, and in Google Play Games if you're signed in.
  • The game uses Google services for adverts, in-app purchases and sign-in. Those services collect some device information, described below.
  • We never sell your information.

Information stored on your device

Your save (career progress, currencies, purchases, settings) and a randomly generated staff ID (e.g. SX-7K2Q-41) are kept on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them, unless you have a cloud save in Google Play Games.

Google Play Games (optional sign-in and cloud saves)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the game with your Play Games player name and ID, and your progress is saved to Google's Saved Games so it can move between phones. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows adverts through Google AdMob: optional rewarded adverts you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen advert between promotions. To serve and measure adverts and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with adverts.

If you're in the UK, EEA or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any advert loads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → More → Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). VIP players see no adverts. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation from Google so it can deliver what you bought and restore it later.

Game content updates

The game may download updated game content (events, prices, text) from a web server. As with any web request, that server sees your IP address, but no personal information is sent and we don't use it to identify you.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game shows reminders (for example, when your in-tray is full). These are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or your phone's settings.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we'll help.

Why information is used (legal bases)

  • Consent – personalised advertising, where required.
  • Contract – delivering and restoring in-app purchases, and cloud saves you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests – keeping the game working, showing non-personalised adverts, and preventing fraud.

How long information is kept

Data on your phone stays until you clear it or uninstall the game. Information held by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict how it's used, and withdraw consent at any time. Because we don't hold personal data on our own systems, most requests are best made through the in-game settings above or to Google directly, but we're happy to help. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
