1. The basics

You start as the Work Experience Kid making tea in the basement kitchen of Synergex plc. Every floor of the 30-storey tower has its own job, a machine or desk that works on a timer and pays out whenever it finishes. Your aim is to earn, upgrade and get promoted all the way to the top floor.

Your first job: making the tea. Tap it to hurry it along.

  • Jobs earn on their own. Once a job is started, it keeps going, even while the app is closed.
  • Level jobs up to earn more and to fill the desks with staff.
  • Fill the Office Kudos bar at the top of the screen, then tap it to ask for a promotion.
  • Each promotion boosts everything you earn, and every few promotions you move up a floor to a new job.

On your first day, Brenda from HR shows you round, so you can also learn as you go.

Golden paperclipsThe premium currency. Earned from promotions, challenges, your attendance card, events and the free-paperclips ad, or bought in the Shop. Spend them on boosts, time skips and premium tools.

3. Getting around the office

The main screen is one floor of the tower, seen from the side. Drag left and right to walk along it and pinch to zoom. Almost everything can be tapped.

  1. The floor's job. Tap it to hurry it. Its tag at the bottom of the screen shows its level, what it earns and the Level up button.
  2. Your staff. Desks fill up as the job levels up. Tap anyone for a quip.
  3. Your desk (marked YOU) is on the floor you've been promoted to.
  4. The manager's office. Tap the manager to hear what they think.
  5. The lifts. Tap a lift for the button panel and travel to any floor you've reached.
  6. The printer and water cooler. Tap them for a surprise.
  7. The fire escape. Keep an eye on the stairwell: people come and go.

The map button in the top bar (the little tower) shows the whole building: the floors you've unlocked, where your desk is, and who runs each floor.

The lights follow your phone's clock: day from 6am, night from 7pm.

4. The foyer

The ground floor is the foyer. Take the lift to G to visit.

  1. The lifts back up to the offices.
  2. The clock-in machine. Tap it to see your attendance card and streak (see Clocking in).
  3. The noticeboard. While an event is running, a ribbon saying EVENT runs across its corner: tap the board for the event. The rest of the time it's covered in the usual office notices.
  4. The "Event soon!" poster. It appears five days before an event starts. Tap it to see what's coming.
  5. Maxine on reception, always on the phone.
  6. Ron from security. He waves.
  7. Stan the janitor. Tap him for About: the app's version, what's new, and licences.
  8. The way in. Visitors arrive through the revolving door.

5. Jobs and levels

  • Start a job with its first level, then level it up with cash. Each level earns more.
  • At levels 10, 25, 50, 100 and beyond, the job works twice as fast.
  • Tap a job to hurry it. Every tap pushes the current batch along.
  • In the Tasks window you can buy one level, ten, or MAX (as many as you can afford) at once.
Tip: new floors' jobs earn much more than old ones, so put most of your cash into your newest job, and pick up the cheap milestone levels on older ones.

6. Promotions and floors

  • There are 150 promotions, five on each of the 30 floors.
  • When the Office Kudos bar is full it flashes: tap it to ask for a promotion.
  • Every promotion multiplies all cash (×1.10) and Office Kudos (×1.06).
  • The first promotion on each floor moves your desk up a floor, unlocks that floor's job and pays golden paperclips. A new manager introduces themselves.
  • The 2nd and 4th promotions on a floor unlock its tools.
  • Your outfit gets smarter the higher you climb.

The floors run from Support (tea, post, photocopying) through Admin, IT and Facilities, Management and Senior, up to the Executive floors: mergers, golf and, at the very top, Running the Empire.

7. Tasks, Tools, Career and Shop

The four buttons along the bottom open windows over the office:

8. While you're away

  • Your team keeps working while the app is closed, up to your away time: 3 hours to start, up to 24 hours with tools, plus 4 more for VIPs.
  • When you come back, the Back in the office card shows what you earned. Tap Collect, or Double it by watching a short ad (VIPs double it instantly).
  • When your away time runs out, work stops until you return. A reminder lets you know your in-tray is full (turn reminders on in Settings).
  • Keep screen on (in Settings) keeps your phone awake while the game is open, so you can leave the office running in front of you.
  • Pressing Back asks whether you want to clock off. Clocking off saves your game, to the cloud too if you're signed in, before the screen switches off.

9. Clocking in every day

  • Weekly streak bonus: every full week of streak adds +5% to the cash and Kudos rewards, up to +25%. Days 8–14 get +5%, days 15–21 get +10%, and so on. Paperclips stay the same. The card shows your streak and your current bonus.
  • Missed a day? If you've missed two days or fewer, you can keep your streak by watching an ad (VIPs keep it for free), or start again from day 1.
  • A 6:30pm reminder lets you know if today's reward is still waiting.

10. Challenges

There are 50 challenges, shown one at a time by the # button on the right of the screen. Its bar fills as you go, and it flashes CLAIM! when one's done. Each pays golden paperclips, from 3 for the early ones up to 25. They point you towards things you might not have found yet: the lift, the map, coffee breaks, the Tools window. Later ones take days or weeks.

11. Events

  • Events run for a set time with bonus jobs and boosts, such as double Kudos or ×1.5 cash.
  • Look for the "Event soon!" poster in the foyer five days before one starts, and the event button on the right while it's running.
  • Some events run one stage a day. Finish each day's stage on its day: a missed stage locks, and 5 paperclips reopen it until the event ends. Finish every stage to win a trophy for your desk.
  • Event jobs are priced by your own pay, so they're fair at any rank.

At Christmas the whole office gets dressed up: snow, tinsel and lights, festive jumpers, a tree in the foyer, and Santa flies past the windows.

12. Coffee breaks, ads and VIP

  • Coffee break: watch a short ad and everything runs twice as fast for 15 minutes. Breaks stack up to 4 hours.
  • Free paperclips: watch an ad for 3 golden paperclips, up to three times a day.
  • Ads are always your choice, up to 10 rewarded ads a day. There's also a short ad after every third promotion.
  • VIP (a one-off purchase) removes ads for good, gives you ad rewards instantly, saves broken streaks for free, adds 4 hours of away time, and gives you a gold lanyard.

13. Settings and cloud saves

The cog at the top right opens Settings:

  • Account: sign in with Google Play Games to save your career to the cloud. If you get a new phone, sign in there and choose Load from cloud.
  • Sound: sound effects, office background noise and title music, each with its own volume. At the bottom are Vibration and Job payout SFX (the coin bleep).
  • Game: reminders, and keep screen on.
  • More: this handbook, language, restore purchases, privacy settings and the privacy policy.

14. Questions

Does the game keep earning when it's closed?

Yes, up to your away time (3 hours to start). Raise it with tools, or with VIP.

I bought something and it didn't arrive.

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Google account.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On your old phone, sign in to Google Play Games in Settings and tap Save now. On the new phone, sign in with the same account and tap Load from cloud.

Can I turn the ads off?

Rewarded ads only play when you choose. VIP removes all ads, including the one after every third promotion.

Something looks wrong, or I've found a bug.

Email dialupstudios.games@gmail.com. Stan in the foyer will tell you which version you're on.
